The Three Technical Tiers

A progressive ladder moving from factual grounding to structured fine-tuning and multi-agent consensus.

Level 1: Grounding

RAG & Knowledge Graphs

Analogy: Library / Open-Book Exam

  • Retrieves external authoritative medical knowledge to eliminate hallucinations.
  • Integrates LightRAG or Graph RAG to connect disease, symptom, and treatment entities.
  • Ensures diagnoses are verified against real-time clinical guidelines.
Level 2: Specialization

Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

Analogy: Teaching & Mentoring

  • Adapts small multimodal models via LoRA and QLoRA in 4-bit precision.
  • Trains the network to produce structured Top-5 ranked differential diagnoses.
  • Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) on clinical rationale.
Level 3: Collaboration

Multi-Agent Systems

Analogy: Clinical Board Consultation

  • Implements the MedAgents framework to replace single isolated model reasoning.
  • Dedicated specialist agents for radiology imagery, lab vitals, and symptom history.
  • Lead agent coordinates structured debate to pinpoint subtle disease markers.

End-to-End Diagnostic Pipeline

How multimodal inputs flow through the local system to produce patient recommendations.

Step 1: Ingestion

Multimodal Inputs

Unstructured clinical narratives, patient demographics, travel history, vitals, accompanied by diagnostic imaging (X-rays, CTs, MRIs, pathology slides).

Step 2: Processing

Reasoning & Retrieval

Entities extracted to query the medical Knowledge Graph. Vision-Language backbones process imagery, while specialist agents debate differential options.

Step 3: Recommendation

Structured Differential

Ranked Top-5 candidate diseases with explicit reasoning steps, clinical caveats, and recommended confirmatory laboratory tests.

Why Build Local Small Models?

Four core advantages over relying on proprietary commercial cloud APIs.

1. HIPAA & Privacy

Protected Health Information (PHI) cannot be dispatched to external third-party cloud servers. Local instances keep data strictly inside hospital boundaries.

2. Deep Customization

Direct control over tokenizer vocabularies, system prompts, quantization schemes, and adapter weights tailored for rare tropical conditions.

3. Zero Ongoing API Costs

Commercial multimodal token billing scales unsustainably in production. Local open-weight architectures eliminate API dependency.

4. High Employability

Industry recruiters (Hitachi, Katalon, etc.) prize AI engineers who can quantize, fine-tune, benchmark, and deploy local inference engines (vLLM, Ollama).
